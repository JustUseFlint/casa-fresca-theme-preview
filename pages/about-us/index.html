About Casa Fresca

Every meal tells a story

Casa Fresca is Sergio Landaverde’s kitchen. He grew up in El Salvador surrounded by the aromas of fresh pupusas, tamales and stews, and brought that love of cooking to the United States.

Sergio’s story

From El Salvador, by way of fine-dining kitchens

The training

Years in top restaurant kitchens

Sergio worked as a career chef in high-end restaurants, from mastering knife work to perfecting complex dishes. That is where the precision comes from.

The leap

Something of his own

He started Casa Fresca to blend that professional training with the soul of the food he grew up with, and to build a better life for his wife and three children.

Today

A family business, every week

Meals made every week, coolers stocked around town, and catering for everything from rehearsal dinners to company dinners for 160.

Our mission

“To deliver delicious, high-quality meals that nourish the body and soul.”
