Questions

Good to know

When do I need to order by?

Thursday at 6 PM for meals the following Tuesday. The countdown at the top of the site always shows the next cut-off.

When do I get my meals?

Tuesday, either delivered to your door or ready in the cooler at Ironsmith's Gym, 1714 E Main St, Crawfordsville.

Does the menu change?

Every week. A new menu goes up each week, and favourites come back around.

How do I heat a meal?

Keep meals refrigerated. Microwave for 2 to 3 minutes and enjoy.

Are the meals frozen?

No. Everything is chef-prepared with fresh, never-frozen ingredients.

Can I add a note to my order?

Yes. Open your cart and use "Order special instructions", or call or text (317) 294-2433.
